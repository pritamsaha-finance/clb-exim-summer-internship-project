##Pritam Saha

**MBA Finance | PGPFM @ BIBS | Aspiring Financial Analyst**

This repository showcases my Summer Internship Project at **CLB EXIM Pvt. Ltd.**, where I conducted a case study on Sustainability & Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) in International Trade within the mica export industry.
The project involved analyzing business operations, trade data, CSR initiatives, supply-chain challenges, and strategic recommendations aligned with international sustainability frameworks.

##Key Skills Demonstrated

- Business & Financial Research
- Data Analysis & Insight Generation
- Trade & Export Market Analysis
- Corporate Sustainability (CSR & ESG)
- Risk Assessment & Strategic Planning
- Stakeholder Analysis
- Supply Chain & Traceability Analysis
- Report Writing
- Presentation Design (PowerPoint)
- Business Communication

##Project Highlights

- Analyzed CLB EXIM's export business and market positioning.
- Evaluated ethical sourcing, environmental practices, governance, and employee welfare.
- Studied UN Global Compact, UN SDGs, and ILO guidelines.
- Identified implementation challenges and developed a 12–24 month strategic roadmap for sustainable growth.
